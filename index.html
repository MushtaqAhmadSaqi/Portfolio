01 Education · Exam prep · Flagship

COMSATSPrepHub

Verified past papers, practice quizzes and score tracking for COMSATS students, in one place.

  • Past papers checked and sorted by subject, so you're not guessing which one is right.
  • Quizzes that show you where you're weak, not just what you scored.

Under the hood: quiz progress is saved in your browser, so a refresh doesn't wipe your attempt.

03 Utility · Flask

MultiVid

Paste a link, pick a quality, download.

  • Shows title, thumbnail and duration before you download.
  • Choose your quality, or pull MP3 audio with FFmpeg.

Under the hood: a local utility for permitted downloads, with runtime output and media caches git-ignored.
